|en=Unattributed, very detailed, two page colored edition (1604?), copy of the 1602 map ''[[:en:Kunyu Wanguo Quantu|Kunyu Wanguo Quantu]]'' by [[:en:Matteo Ricci|Matteo Ricci]] at the request of the [[:en:Wanli Emperor|Wanli Emperor]]. This digitalization of the map is of a Japanese export copy of the original Chinese version, with phonetic annotations in [[:en:Katakana|Katakana]] for foreign place names outside of the Sinic world, predominantly around Europe, Russia and the Near East. Note that 金島 [Kinshima, Island of Gold] in its entirety and the label 銀島 [Ginshima, Island of Silver] have been added to Ricci's original.
